How to read this

Rainbet’s Dice page advertises 99% RTP. In this simplified model, a win chance of p% pays a total multiplier of approximately 99 ÷ p. The expected net result is therefore about −1% of each dollar wagered over many rounds, not a forecast for any one session.

This is an educational model based on Rainbet’s published figures, not a verified copy of its live game. RTP, game rules, rounding, and availability may differ. No strategy can be inferred from this simulator that guarantees a profit.
